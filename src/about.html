The Challenge

Understanding Dyslexia

Dyslexia makes it difficult to identify speech sounds and understand how they correspond to letters and words. Current training methods require trained practitioners, are traditionally conducted in therapy settings, and are mostly available only in English.

Difficulty in School

Struggles with reading and learning affect academic performance

Behavioural Issues

Frustration from learning difficulties can lead to behavioural challenges

Low Self-Esteem

Repeated struggles can diminish confidence and self-worth

Loneliness

Children may feel isolated due to their learning differences

Our Solution

Building the Future of Dyslexia Intervention

Readabled is a clinically validated online application developed by Hermit Labs Pvt. Ltd. that provides a wide variety of phonetic learning exercises. Our initial offline modules were developed and validated at AIIMS Delhi.

Our platform features Phoneme & Syllable Breakdown, Multilingual Support, Gamified Interactive Practice, Instant Pronunciation Feedback, a Chrome Browser Extension, and Document Analysis capabilities.

By integrating neuroscience, artificial intelligence, and human-centered design, we deliver measurable therapeutic and cognitive outcomes that help children achieve their full potential.

Our Mission

To create evidence-based, clinically informed solutions that cater to individuals with neurodevelopmental and sensory disorders. We leverage cutting-edge technology to provide accessible, effective interventions that make a real difference in children's lives.

Our Vision

To advance digital therapeutics and contribute to accessible, data-driven healthcare solutions globally. We envision a world where every child with dyslexia has access to personalized, scientifically-backed learning tools that unlock their true potential.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Digital Intervention

Our platform combines clinical expertise with cutting-edge AI technology to deliver effective learning outcomes.

Phoneme & Syllable Breakdown

Structured phoneme learning with word sounds categorized by phonemes for systematic learning.

Multilingual Support

Currently supporting English with Hindi and regional languages under development.

Gamified Interactive Practice

Engaging educational games and story-based modules for interactive learning experiences.

Instant Pronunciation Feedback

AI-powered speech recognition providing real-time feedback on pronunciation accuracy.

Chrome Browser Extension

Practice reading anywhere on the web. Click any word to practice with multilingual support.

Document Analyzer

Upload any document for analysis, practice mouth movements, and AI-based document interaction.

Admin Dashboard

Educator dashboard to onboard users, track progress, monitor performance, and adjust settings.

Remote Monitoring

Home-based therapy with comprehensive remote monitoring capabilities for parents and clinicians.
